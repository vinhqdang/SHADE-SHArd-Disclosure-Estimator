\section{Experiments}
\subsection{The No-Free-Lunch Theorem in RAG Partitioning}
As illustrated in Figure \ref{fig:baseline_comparison}, blindly maximizing semantic coherence (as K-Means or GraphRAG does) yields a catastrophic disclosure penalty, as entire conceptual topics are localized to single shards. Conversely, privacy-first techniques like VertiSplitRAG or DPVoteRAG destroy semantic utility. This fundamental inverse correlation establishes the No-Free-Lunch theorem for RAG partitioning.
