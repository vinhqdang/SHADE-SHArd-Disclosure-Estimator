\section{Methodology}
\subsection{The SHArd Disclosure Estimator (SHADE)}
To quantify the risk of an adversary reconstructing a topic from a compromised shard, we introduce the SHArd Disclosure Estimator (SHADE). SHADE penalizes partitions that co-locate semantically adjacent documents. Given a similarity graph where edge weights represent the cosine similarity between document embeddings, SHADE calculates the ratio of intra-shard edges to total graph edges. A high SHADE score indicates massive privacy leakage.

\subsection{The CoShard Pipeline}
Our proposed technique, CoShard, explicitly optimizes the privacy-utility tradeoff. We first construct a K-Nearest Neighbor (K-NN) similarity graph over the document embedding space. We then apply a modified, two-phase community detection algorithm (based on Leiden modularity maximization), explicitly penalized by the SHADE metric weighted by a hyperparameter $\lambda$.
